# Quickstart

```text
# 1. Route anything through the front desk
/hq a vendor hasn't paid us for 4 months

# 2. Or go straight to a leader
/cfo what are my compliance deadlines for the next 90 days?
/cmo launch SWAFTO to kirana stores in Bhubaneswar, 60 days, small budget
/redteam full pre-launch sweep of SWAFTO backend on staging

# 3. Split a big goal across the whole company
/ceo launch SWAFTO in 60 days — involve product, tech, marketing, sales, finance and ops;
     give me one integrated plan with owners and the decisions I need to make

# 4. Make the system better over time
/evolve            # propose skill upgrades from real usage (you approve)
```

Full templates: [prompt-templates.md](prompt-templates.md).

Rules that always hold: you approve anything external, financial, legal, or irreversible; agents produce drafts and plans; nothing is sent, signed, paid, or deployed without you.
