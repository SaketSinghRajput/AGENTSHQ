# Marketing Compliance India

Checklist: claims substantiated · comparative claims fair · disclosures visible · no fake reviews/testimonials/scarcity · consent for contact data · DND compliance · platform policies · trademark/copyright cleared for images, music, fonts · category restrictions (alcohol advertising is prohibited in India; surrogate ads restricted). Output pass/fix list. Guidance only — flag legal questions for a lawyer.
