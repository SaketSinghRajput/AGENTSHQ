# Brand Identity

Brand platform: purpose, promise, personality (3-5 traits with do/don't), voice & tone chart, naming rules. Visual brief: logo direction, palette with contrast checks, type, imagery style. Check trademark availability (IP India search) before committing to a name — flag for a lawyer.
