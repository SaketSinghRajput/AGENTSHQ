# Paid Acquisition

- Never launch or spend without founder approval of budget; start with small test budgets.
- Conversion tracking verified before spend (pixel/CAPI/GA4 events).
- Campaign structure, audiences, 3+ creatives per ad set, daily caps, kill rules (e.g. CPA > 2× target after X spend).
- Weekly report: spend, CPA, ROAS, learnings. Follow platform ad policies and ASCI codes.
