# Lifecycle Crm

- Only contact people who opted in; honour unsubscribe; respect TRAI DND/commercial-communication rules for SMS/calls and WhatsApp Business policies.
- Journeys: welcome, activation nudges, nurture, re-engagement; each with trigger, message, timing, goal metric.
- Keep personal data minimal (DPDP Act).
