# Copywriting

- One reader, one message, one action. Lead with the customer's problem in their words.
- Give 3 variants for headlines/CTAs for testing.
- Every claim needs proof (number, testimonial with permission, demo). No fake testimonials, fake scarcity or unverifiable superlatives ("India's #1") without substantiation.
