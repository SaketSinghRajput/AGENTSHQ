# Go To Market Launch

- Launch tier (1 major / 2 feature / 3 minor) decides effort.
- T-30 → T+30 calendar: assets, owners, approvals, channels.
- Launch-day runbook: posting order, monitoring, response templates, rollback for broken links.
- Success metrics defined before launch.
