# Customer Insights

- ICP: firmographics/demographics, triggers, budget owner, buying process.
- Interview guide (past behaviour, not hypotheticals); synthesize into JTBD, pains, gains, exact customer words.
- Mine public reviews/forums of competitors. Note language preferences (Hindi/Odia/English) and channel habits (WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram).
