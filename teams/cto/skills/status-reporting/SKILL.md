# Status Reporting

Update `docs/STATUS.md` and reply with:
- One line per project: stage, % to next gate, RAG (green/amber/red).
- Done since last report (with evidence).
- Blockers needing the founder (decisions, money, hardware).
- Next 3 actions with owners.
Keep it under 15 lines.
