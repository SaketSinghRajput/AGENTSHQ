# Privacy Compliance India

- Data inventory: what personal data, why, where stored, retention, who accesses.
- Consent notices in plain language; purpose limitation; deletion/correction flows.
- Breach response plan. Cross-border transfer check.
- Hardware: list applicable certifications and test labs; budget and timeline.
- This is guidance, not legal advice; flag items needing a lawyer.
