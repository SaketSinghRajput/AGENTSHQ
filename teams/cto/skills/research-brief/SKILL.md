# Research Brief

Output `docs/research/<topic>.md`:
1. Question and why it matters.
2. Sources: primary first (papers, datasheets, standards, official docs, repos). Every claim cites a URL. Mark unverified items.
3. Options table: approach | maturity | cost | risk | fit.
4. Competitors / prior products (India + global) with pricing if public.
5. Recommendation + what would change it.
6. Open questions for experiments.
Never fabricate citations or numbers.
