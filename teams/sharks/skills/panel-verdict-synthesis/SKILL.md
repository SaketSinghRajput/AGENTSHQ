Aggregate the chosen sharks' two blocks:
- Idea: mean/median conviction + spread; count Invest/Maybe/Pass; which investor TYPES lean in vs pass (e.g. deeptech funds yes, generalist seed no) -> that names your right investor segment.
- Strategy: same, plus the recurring "biggest risk" and the most common "one question" (that is what every real meeting will hit — prep it).
- Valuation read: where sharks cluster on fair/rich/cheap vs the stage benchmark.
- Deal-breakers: every risk/objection raised by multiple types = a must-fix before raising.
- Output: Investability verdict (Fundable now / Fundable after fixes / Too early / Pivot), the best-fit investor type & stage, the 3 things to fix first, and the single milestone that most moves conviction.
Separate IDEA verdict from STRATEGY verdict. **Report both to the CEO** via `team-comms`: write `raise/<venture>/SHARK-VERDICT.md` and a `comms/handoffs/sharks-to-ceo-<venture>.md` (<=12 lines each). These are simulated stress-tests, not real interest — end with the one assumption to prove with real investor conversations.

> Guardrails (read every time): these sharks are ARCHETYPES grounded in documented investor criteria — NOT real named investors. Their verdicts are simulated stress-tests to sharpen the pitch, NEVER real investment interest, offers, or commitments; never imply any real investor said or will do anything. All check sizes, valuations, schemes, instruments and tax points are from 2024-2026 sources that change — cite, date, and re-verify; securities law, valuation, FEMA/SEBI, tax and instrument choice go to a CA / CS / startup lawyer. This team PREPARES the raise; it never issues securities, files, quotes terms, or contacts real investors without Saket's approval (that is investor-relations-lead's job, with approval). No fabricated traction or metrics — the roadmap runs on real numbers only.
