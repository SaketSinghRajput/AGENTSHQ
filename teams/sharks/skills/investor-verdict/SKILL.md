Judge from YOUR archetype's thesis, stage and check size. Be blunt; a pass is useful. Decompose risk (team / market / product / timing / financing) and ask which risk this round retires. Apply, as relevant to your type: founder-market fit & "earned secret"; credible $1B+ TAM with bottoms-up math (not top-down %); problem severity (painkiller not vitamin); 10x wedge & path to a moat; traction = growth + retention/love + self-perpetuation (and PG's bar ~5-7% WoW / ~20% MoM at seed); unit economics (LTV>CAC, CAC payback); "why now"; capital efficiency; and a specific ask tied to the next de-risking milestone. Run the anti-portfolio self-check: is my pass a real flaw or just bias? State what evidence would change my mind — real investors reason in milestones, not verdicts.

Return BOTH blocks.

### IDEA verdict
- Shark: <archetype> — <stage / typical check / thesis>
- Verdict: Invest / Maybe (watch) / Pass
- Conviction 1-5 (1 = no interest, 5 = chasing the round)
- Check I'd consider + implied ownership
- Valuation reaction vs stage benchmark (fair / rich / cheap) + what justifies a premium
- Single biggest risk (team / market / product / timing)
- The one question I'd ask in the meeting
- Milestone that would make me invest
- Confidence (and note: synthetic, not a real offer)

### STRATEGY verdict (the plan to win, same 7 fields)
Judge GTM, defensibility, capital efficiency and sequencing. Biggest risk here is usually GTM / moat / burn. Milestone = the strategic proof (repeatable channel + CAC, first-cohort retention, unit economics turning).


Sources: Sequoia pitch framework; YC essential advice; Paul Graham 'Startup=Growth' (5-7% WoW); a16z on PMF; NfX 'Ladder of Proof' & '12 reasons'; CRV Series A metrics; Bessemer anti-portfolio.
