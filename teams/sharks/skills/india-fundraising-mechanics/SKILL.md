**Instruments:** priced equity via CCPS (the Indian default; full investor rights; conversion within 20 yrs; not a taxable transfer); iSAFE (100X.VC standard form — legally a CCPS, lightweight; read the actual subscription agreement); convertible note (DPIIT-only, ₹25L min/investor, up to 10-yr window, reported on Form CN; FC-GPR on conversion); CCD. A US post-money SAFE has no clean Indian shelf.
**Private placement (Companies Act s.42):** board + special resolution -> registered-valuer report (s.247; for foreign investors price >= FMV, merchant-banker/CA cert <=90 days) -> PAS-4 offer letter -> money to a separate account, allot within 60 days -> PAS-3 within 30 days; <=200 persons/FY.
**FEMA/FC-GPR:** foreign issuance filed on RBI FIRMS within 30 days of allotment; late-submission fees apply; annual FLA return.
**SEBI AIF:** funds register as AIFs (Cat I incl. VCF & Angel Funds). 2025 Angel Fund overhaul (accredited investors only; first close 12 mo; ₹25Cr follow-on cap) — new, confirm with counsel.
**Angel tax:** s.56(2)(viib) abolished effective ~FY2024-25 (AY2025-26) — confirm year/scope with a CA; valuation substance and s.68 still matter.
**Non-dilutive:** SISFS (grant up to ₹20L / up to ₹50L convertible; DPIIT, <=2 yrs, via approved incubator), SIDBI Fund-of-Funds (into AIFs, not direct), Startup Odisha + state schemes (verify current terms).
**Close docs:** term sheet -> DD -> SSA + SHA -> approvals + AoA amendment -> allotment + money in -> PAS-3 / MGT-14 / SH-1 / FC-GPR.
Everything here changes and is deal-specific: confirm with a CA, CS and startup lawyer before acting.
