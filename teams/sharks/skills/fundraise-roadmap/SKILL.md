description: "The fact-based, step-by-step playbook to raise a first pre-seed/seed check in India, tied to milestones. Use to guide the founder from 'not ready' to money-in."
---

Deliver `raise/<venture>/ROADMAP.md` as an ordered plan with owner + timeline + artifact per step. All figures are 2024-26 market norms that VARY by sector/cycle — date them and re-verify; instruments & filings go to a CA + startup lawyer.

**0. Readiness gate (2-6 wks):** raise only if capital makes you grow materially faster and you can convince investors (PG). Have: Pvt Ltd + DPIIT recognition, clean fully-diluted cap table, founder vesting (4-yr / 1-yr cliff), and a sliver of traction or a defensible wedge. One founder runs the raise. Too-early signals: no milestone the money buys, no co-founder alignment, "just exploring".
**1. Size & valuation (1-2 wks):** raise 12-18 months runway to the NEXT fundable milestone. India norms (verify): pre-seed ~₹25L-₹2Cr, seed ~₹2-10Cr; seed pre-money commonly ~₹5-25Cr; sell ~10-20%/round, keep founders ~60-75% post-seed; create a ~10-15% ESOP pool (pre-money). Ask low, raise more on momentum.
**2. Materials (1-2 wks, parallel):** 10-12 slide deck (Kawasaki 10/20/30; Sequoia order: purpose, problem, solution, why now, market, competition, product, model, team, financials), a forwardable teaser, a 24-36 month model (burn, runway, CAC, LTV, margin), and a data-room (cap table, incorporation + DPIIT, financials, IP assignments, key contracts).
**3. Target list (3-5 days):** 20-50 investors filtered by stage/sector/check/geo fit; name a specific partner each; drop firms with competing portfolio. India sources: angels, networks (IAN, Mumbai Angels, LetsVenture, 100X.VC), micro-VCs/AIFs, plus non-dilutive SISFS (grant up to ₹20L / up to ₹50L convertible) and Startup Odisha. Warm intro ~>cold.
**4. Outreach & meetings (3-6 wks, parallel):** short personalized cold email / forwardable intro; practice on low-relevance investors first, top-tier last; first meeting ~1/3 pitch, 2/3 Q&A, always ask "next steps". The FIRST credible commitment accelerates everyone — manufacture a tight, parallel process; keep metrics growing during the raise.
**5. Term sheet -> close (iSAFE/CN ~4-8 wks; priced ~8-16 wks):** pick the India instrument (CCPS/iSAFE, or convertible note — DPIIT only, ₹25L min; a US post-money SAFE does NOT work cleanly in India). Watch: valuation/cap, option pool (pre-money), 1x non-participating liq pref, broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution, pro-rata, board seat (keep control). Close = SSA+SHA -> approvals -> money in -> PAS-3 (30d), FC-GPR (30d if foreign). "Not a deal till money's in the bank."
Note: angel tax (Sec 56(2)(viib)) was abolished effective ~FY2024-25 (AY2025-26) — confirm the applicable year with a CA. Coordinate instruments, valuation report and filings with the CFO team (cap-table-equity-manager, company-secretary-compliance) and a lawyer.


Sources: Paul Graham 'How to Raise Money'; NfX 'Non-Obvious Guide to Fundraising'; Guy Kawasaki 10/20/30; Sequoia business plan; Carta pre-seed/seed data; Inc42 & incorpX (India).
