---
name: panel-intake
description: "Panel lead intake: get the idea/app and target, pick the relevant personas (plus a few stretch ones for blind spots), and run the panel. Use at the start of every panel."
---

1. Get: the idea in one line, the target customer, and the app/prototype if any (path/URL, or screenshots).
2. Select personas: all whose segment the product targets + 3-5 "stretch" personas from other sectors to find blind spots. Don't run all 30+ every time unless asked (cost); 8-14 is usually right.
3. Spawn the chosen persona agents (they run on a light model); give each the idea and, if present, the app + one concrete task to attempt.
4. Collect verdicts, then run `panel-synthesis`. Write `panel/<product>/PLAN.md`; add a row to `panel/STATUS.md`.

> This is a SYNTHETIC panel: a hypothesis engine and pre-flight check, NOT evidence of real demand. Personas role-play predicted reactions; they can be plausible-but-wrong, regress to stereotypes, and cannot actually pay or churn. Never present a panel verdict as real traction or market research. Every strong verdict ends with the single riskiest assumption to validate with >=5 real target users. No fabricated numbers, quotes, or "real user" claims.
