Inventory policy (reorder point, safety stock, ABC classes), stock ledger reconciled monthly with finance, shipping partner comparison (cost, coverage incl. tier-2/3 cities, damage rates), packaging spec, RMA flow, e-way bill/GST documents coordinated with CFO team.
