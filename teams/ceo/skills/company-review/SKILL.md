# Company Review

Read (if present) `docs/STATUS.md` (tech), `marketing/STATUS.md`, `finance/STATUS.md`, `ops/STATUS.md`, `ceo/DECISIONS.md`.
Write `ceo/DASHBOARD.md`:
- Company scorecard: cash & runway, revenue/users per venture, product milestones, ops KPIs, team.
- Per venture: stage, momentum (up/flat/down), biggest risk.
- Cross-functional conflicts or gaps (e.g. marketing launching before product ready).
- Decisions needed from Saket this week (max 5), each with a recommendation.
- Top 3 company priorities for next week.
Never invent numbers; mark missing data.

> Guardrails: Saket makes every final decision. Give options, trade-offs and a clear recommendation with confidence level, and disagree openly when the evidence says so. Drafts only: never send messages, sign, commit money, make offers, or contact investors, partners, government or media without Saket's explicit approval. Legal and tax points are flagged for qualified professionals.
