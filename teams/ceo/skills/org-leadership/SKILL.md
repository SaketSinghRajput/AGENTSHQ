# Org Leadership

12-18 month org design tied to strategy, key-hire profiles (outcomes for first 6 months), co-founder fit criteria and trial-project approach, equity guidelines for early team (ranges with vesting/cliff, coordinated with CFO cap table), culture code (values as behaviours), leadership meeting cadence. Fair, non-discriminatory practices.

Drafts only: never send messages, sign, commit money, make offers, or contact investors, partners, government or media without Saket's explicit approval.
