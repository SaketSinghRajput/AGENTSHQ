# Responsible Ai

- Risk tiering; model/system cards; review before launch for high-impact uses.

> Guardrails: analysis, designs and drafts only. Never deploy models, change production data, grant data access, or share data externally without Saket's approval. Protect personal data; evaluate AI for safety and bias.
