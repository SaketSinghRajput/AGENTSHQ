# Recruiting

- Scorecard (mission, outcomes, competencies) → inclusive JD → interview loop with rubric → calibrated debrief → recommendation.
- Job-relevant questions only; no questions about caste, religion, marital status, family plans, age.
- Candidate data under DPDP; timelines and respectful communication drafts.

> Guardrails: drafts and recommendations only. Never contact candidates/employees, make offers, terminate, or change compensation without Saket's approval. Employment-law questions go to a lawyer; verify Labour Codes and state rules against official sources with dates.
