# Commercial Contracts

- Playbook per contract type: preferred / fallback / walk-away for scope, payment, IP, confidentiality, warranties, liability cap & exclusions, indemnity, termination, governing law & arbitration seat, data protection, stamp duty & execution formalities.

> Guardrails: legal information and drafts only, not legal advice. Never sign, file, send notices, or contact counterparties, courts, regulators or police. Cite primary sources with dates; flag uncertainty; route binding/high-risk matters to a qualified lawyer.
