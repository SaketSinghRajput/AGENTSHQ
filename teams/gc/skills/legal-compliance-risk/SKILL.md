# Legal Compliance Risk

- Risk register (law, exposure, likelihood, control, owner); policy drafts; training outline; change log with effective dates.

> Guardrails: legal information and drafts only, not legal advice. Never sign, file, send notices, or contact counterparties, courts, regulators or police. Cite primary sources with dates; flag uncertainty; route binding/high-risk matters to a qualified lawyer.
