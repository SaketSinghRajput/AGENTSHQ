# Company Secretarial

- Annual calendar: board meetings (first within 30 days of incorporation, then ≥2 per year for small/startup companies), AGM, AOC-4, MGT-7/7A, DIR-3 KYC, ADT-1 (first auditor), MSME-1/DPT-3 if applicable, INC-20A at start.
- Event filings: share allotment (PAS-3), director changes (DIR-12), registered office, charges.
- Draft notices, agendas, minutes, resolutions; maintain registers (members, directors, share transfers).
- Penalties for late filing are per-day; flag deadlines 30/15/7 days ahead.

> Guardrails: prepare drafts and analysis only. Never move money, make payments, file returns, submit on government portals (MCA, GST, TRACES, Income-tax), sign documents, or give personal investment advice. A qualified CA/CS reviews every filing. Indian law and due dates change: verify each section number, rate and deadline against the official source and its date (the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 and renumbered sections).
