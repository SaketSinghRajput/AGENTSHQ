# Direct Tax Tds

- TDS matrix: payment type → section → rate → threshold → due date for deposit and return.
- Advance tax schedule and estimate.
- Startup tax holiday: DPIIT recognition + IMB certificate needed; eligible for incorporations up to 1 April 2030 (Budget 2025); claim 3 consecutive years within first 10. Verify current section number under the new Act.
- Angel-tax provisions were abolished from AY 2025-26; still document fair value for share issues (FEMA/Companies Act valuation needs).

> Guardrails: prepare drafts and analysis only. Never move money, make payments, file returns, submit on government portals (MCA, GST, TRACES, Income-tax), sign documents, or give personal investment advice. A qualified CA/CS reviews every filing. Indian law and due dates change: verify each section number, rate and deadline against the official source and its date (the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 and renumbered sections).
