# Finance Review Gate

Write `finance/<slug>/gates/<gate>.md`: sources used · reconciliation checks (bank = books, sub-ledgers = GL, totals tie) · assumptions · open items · reviewer (CA/CS for filings) · founder approval. No external sharing (investors, banks, government) without founder approval.

> Guardrails: prepare drafts and analysis only. Never move money, make payments, file returns, submit on government portals (MCA, GST, TRACES, Income-tax), sign documents, or give personal investment advice. A qualified CA/CS reviews every filing. Indian law and due dates change: verify each section number, rate and deadline against the official source and its date (the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 and renumbered sections).
